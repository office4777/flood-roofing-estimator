About

Written by roofers, on real jobs.

RoofMap came out of Flood Roofing, a working roofing company in Northland. It exists because quoting a re-roof took an afternoon of tape measures and spreadsheets, and because the material order was never quite right.

The problem it was built for

Quoting a re-roof properly means measuring a roof you cannot safely walk, working every sloped length back through the pitch, counting flashings by type and by piece, and turning all of that into a number a homeowner will say yes to. Done by hand it takes most of a day. Done quickly it comes out wrong, and the wrong part is usually the flashings.

The order was the worse half. Back-trays left off. A chimney's flashing set found on the roof rather than on the docket. A sheet run measured to the wrong gutter and cut two metres long. None of it catastrophic on its own, all of it a truck movement and half a day.

Why a roofing company wrote software

Because the products that existed were either measuring services that sell you a report per roof and stop there, or job-management systems that handle everything except the roof itself. Neither knew what a change-of-pitch flashing was, or that a shared edge between two roofs should be counted once.

Every feature in RoofMap earned its place on a real job first. The cut lists match how a roof is actually laid because they were built by the people laying it. The app works with one hand on a windy roof because that is where it gets used.

What that means for you

  • Colorsteel®, Zincalume®, ColorCote® and Marley products already costed in
  • NZ pitches, NZ profiles, NZ suppliers, GST throughout
  • Coastal corrosion zones and the warranty tiers that go with them
  • Rates that ship as real New Zealand trade rates, so your first quote is a believable number rather than zero — and are yours to replace

The trade-off worth naming

A roofing company selling software to other roofers is unusual, and it is fair to be suspicious of it. Nothing you draw, price or quote is visible to us — the Privacy Policy sets out exactly what is held and who can reach it. The reason we sell it rather than keep it is that a tool used by fifty roofing businesses gets better far faster than one used by ours.

Why is it New Zealand only?

Because the parts that matter are local. Coastal corrosion zones, Colorsteel warranty tiers, the profiles the mills actually roll here, the merchants, 15% GST. Software written for another market gets all of that wrong in ways that are hard to see until an order is short.
