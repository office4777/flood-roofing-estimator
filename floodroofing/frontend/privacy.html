The short version

If you read nothing else, read this.

  • We hold what you put in — your account, your business details, your jobs, your customers' contact details, your drawings, your photos and your prices.
  • We do not sell it, rent it, or use it to advertise to anyone.
  • Your price book is never shown to another subscriber and never shared with a supplier.
  • We use no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. What we measure is ten milestones — did a business set itself up, did it draw a roof — and nothing about your customers.
  • Roof images and supplier price lists go to an AI service only when you press the button that does that, and are not used to train anything.
  • You can get your data out or have it deleted. Ask us and we will do it.

Whose information is whose

Two different relationships, and they matter for who you complain to.

There are two kinds of personal information in RoofMap and we treat them differently.

Yours — we are the agency

Your name, your work email, your business details, and the record of your use of RoofMap. We decide what to do with that, so under the Privacy Act 2020 we are the agency responsible. This policy tells you what we do with it, and your rights in section 11 apply to us directly.

Your customers' — you are the agency

The homeowner's name, site address, phone and email that you type into a job; the photos you take of their roof; the quote you send them. You collected that, for your purposes. We only hold it because you asked us to, and we do nothing with it except run the service for you.

If one of your customers wants to know what you hold about them, or wants it corrected or deleted, that request belongs to you, not to us — you can do all of it in the app. If a customer contacts us directly we will point them back to you and let you know.

What we hold

Four buckets. Everything RoofMap stores is in one of them.

Your account and your business

  • Your name, email address, and a password that is stored hashed — nobody here can read it.
  • Your business name and, once you set it up, your trading details: phone, office email, website, physical address, GST number, logo and any "about us" text you put on a proposal.
  • Who is in your business, what role they have, and outstanding invitations.
  • Your plan, your subscription status, and a trial end date if your account has one.

Your work

  • Jobs: the client's name, the site address, their phone and email if you enter them, and the job number.
  • Roof drawings — outlines, lines, pitches, penetrations, dimensions and the scale you set.
  • Any aerial image or photograph you upload or take through the app.
  • Your price book, labour rates and quote templates.
  • Quotes and proposals, and the record of a customer opening, querying, declining or accepting one, including what they picked and the name they typed when they accepted.
  • Snapshots of previous versions of a job, so a bad edit can be recovered.
  • Who made a job and who sent the material order.

How the product is used

Ten milestones, per business, with a timestamp: signed up, finished setup, opened the sample job, drew a roof, saved a job, saved a price book, sent a quote, had a quote accepted, sent a material order, and — before any of those — asked for early access.

The early-access one is the only milestone recorded before there is an account. It stores the region, the number of roofs quoted a month and the software already in use — the three answers from the request form — and no name, address or email. What you typed into that form is held separately, as contact details, and is covered in section 3.

That is the entire list. There is no page tracking, no session recording, no third-party analytics tag, and no event carries a customer name, an address or a price. It exists to answer one question — do new subscribers actually get anywhere in their first week — and it is deliberately built so it cannot quietly become anything else.

Errors

When something breaks, we record the error message, where in the app it happened, the version of RoofMap, your business name and the email of whoever hit it — so we can ring the right person. Access tokens, passwords and image data are stripped out before an error is stored or sent anywhere.

Our servers also keep ordinary web logs, including IP addresses, for a short period.

Why we hold it

To run the thing you are paying for, and to keep it working.

  • To provide the service — store your jobs, do the sums, produce the job pack, serve the quote link your customer opens, send the emails you ask us to send.
  • To keep your account secure and to know who did what inside your business.
  • To bill you, and to know which plan you are on.
  • To fix it when it breaks — see the errors bucket above.
  • To know whether it is working for new subscribers — the nine milestones, and nothing beyond them.
  • To talk to you about your account: a failed payment, a price change, a security notice, an outage. These are not marketing and you cannot unsubscribe from them while you hold an account.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not build profiles of your customers, and we do not use one subscriber's pricing to inform another's.

What goes to an AI, and when

Two buttons, both yours to press. Nothing goes automatically.

RoofMap uses Anthropic's Claude for exactly two things:

  • Reading a roof outline off an image. When you ask it to trace a roof, the image you are working on is sent, and what comes back is a set of corner coordinates and a suggested pitch.
  • Reading a supplier price list. When you upload a CSV or PDF price list, the file is sent, and what comes back is a list of products, units and prices.

Neither happens on its own. Your jobs, your customers' details, your saved price book and your quotes are never sent to an AI service in the background. Nothing is sent unless you press the button that does it.

Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models. We do not use it to train anything either — we do not train models at all.

Be aware of what you are submitting. An aerial image of a property is personal information about the people who live there, and a supplier price list is commercially sensitive. If you would rather not send either, don't — every measurement in RoofMap can be drawn and calibrated by hand, and every price can be typed in.

Where it is stored

Overseas, on managed infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Your data is held in the [Supabase region] region of Supabase's managed Postgres service, with the backend running on Railway and the frontend on Vercel. That means it is stored outside New Zealand.

All traffic between your browser and our servers, and between our servers and every provider above, is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the hosting provider.

What sits on your device

No tracking cookies. Local storage so the app works on a roof with no signal.

RoofMap sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and carries no third-party tags. What it does keep on your device is:

  • Your sign-in token and which business you are signed in to, so you are not asked to log in every time.
  • Your settings and preferences — panel widths, which tab you were on.
  • A local copy of the job you are working on, including photos, held in your browser's storage so the app keeps working with no signal and sends the work up when the signal returns.

That local copy lives on the device until it syncs. Clearing your browser data, or signing out, removes it — along with any work that had not reached our servers. On a shared or borrowed device, sign out when you are done.

How it is protected

Separation between businesses is enforced by the database, not just by the app.

  • Every job, setting and price book is scoped to the business that owns it, and that separation is enforced at the database with row-level security — not only by the code above it.
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth. Nobody at RoofMap can see one.
  • Sessions are signed tokens that expire, and are checked on every request.
  • Customer quote links are guarded by a long random token, and stop working when the quote is withdrawn or the account is closed.
  • Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to run the service.
  • Errors are monitored, and secrets are stripped before an error is recorded or sent.
  • Changes are covered by an automated test suite that runs on every deployment.

No system is perfect, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. If there is ever a privacy breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, as the Privacy Act 2020 requires.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, and a complaint to the Privacy Commissioner if we get it wrong.

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask us to:

  • Show you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong. Most of it you can correct yourself in Settings.
  • Give you a copy of your data in a usable format, including your jobs.
  • Delete your account and its data.

Email support@roofmap.co.nz. We will confirm we have your request and respond within 20 working days, which is what the Act requires. There is no charge.

Some things we may have to keep even after a deletion request — a billing record we are required to hold for tax, for instance. We will tell you if that applies and why.

Remember the distinction in section 2: if the request is about your customer's information, it comes to you, not to us.

Changes to this policy

Same 30 days' notice as the Terms.

We will update this policy as the product changes. For a change that materially affects how we handle your information — a new provider in section 5, a new purpose in section 4 — we will give at least 30 days' notice by email and change the version and date at the top. Older versions are in the public repository this site is built from, so you can see exactly what changed and when.
