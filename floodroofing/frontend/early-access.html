Early access

We are letting roofers in a batch at a time.

Not because there is a queue for the sake of one. Because a roofer who hits a rough edge with nobody watching tells the whole region, and we would rather get each business set up properly than collect signups.

What happens after you send this

1

We read it, and one of us replies

Not a drip sequence. Aron or someone who has been on a roof reads what you wrote about your setup, and answers from a real address you can reply to.

2

You get an access code and set your business up

About a minute — business name, your name, a password. There is a finished sample job waiting inside, so you can see a completed quote, cut list and material order before you do your own.

3

We load your prices with you

RoofMap ships with real New Zealand trade rates so your first quote is a believable number rather than zero. They are a starting point, not your prices — send us a merchant price list and we will get your own rates in.

Founding roofers: 30% off your first 12 months Businesses who come in during early access pay 30% less for their first 12 months, then the standard rate — $104.30 a month instead of $149 on Solo, $209.30 instead of $299 on Team, $384.30 instead of $549 on Business, all plus GST. It comes off automatically when you subscribe; there is no code to enter.

You also get a direct line to the people building it. In return we ask for honest feedback when something is wrong, which is the entire reason the batches are small.
