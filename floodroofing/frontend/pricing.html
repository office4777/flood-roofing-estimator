Priced per business, not per seat.

What it replaces

The measuring is the part that usually costs.

Overseas roof-measuring services sell you a report per roof — roughly NZ$40 to $65 each, more for anything complicated, and more again if you want it back the same day. Twenty roofs in a month is past a thousand dollars, and you have bought measurements only: no cut list, no order, no quote, and nothing your customer can sign.

RoofMap includes the measuring on every plan, with no cap. The reason it can is that the measuring is not a service somebody performs for you — it is you, tracing the roof, with the software doing the arithmetic. Nobody is sitting in an office drawing your roof, so nobody needs paying per roof.

Questions

The things worth asking before you commit.

Is measuring charged per roof?

No. Measuring is included on every plan, with no cap and no per-report fee. Draw as many roofs as you like, on as many jobs as you like.

Is it priced per user?

No — per business. One subscription covers the company up to the seats on the plan. Solo is one login, Team is up to five, Business is unlimited.

Do I need Fergus or another job management system?

No. RoofMap works on its own: the home board is your job list, and job files hold the council plans, the old quote and anything else that belongs to the job.

If you do run Fergus, jobs, photos and job packs pass between the two. How that works.

What happens to my jobs if I stop subscribing?

They are yours. You can export them, and the Privacy Policy sets out how to ask for a copy or a deletion under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Are the prices in New Zealand dollars?

Yes, and they exclude GST. RoofMap is built for New Zealand roofing only — NZ profiles, NZ steel grades, NZ coastal corrosion zones and 15% GST throughout.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, from Settings, at any time. Adding people moves you up when you pass the seats on your plan; dropping back down is the same screen.
