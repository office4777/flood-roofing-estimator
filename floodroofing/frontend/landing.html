How it works

Draw it once. Everything follows.

The same job that used to mean a tape, a spreadsheet, a biro sketch for the crew and three phone calls to the customer.

Map→Price→Quote→Order

Draw the roof to scale

Trace the outline over the aerial, set one known wall, and every ridge, hip, valley, barge and gutter is measured. The crew gets a plan they can read, not a sketch.

The job pack writes itself

Sheet runs laid out per plane, the flashing schedule, back-trays, fixings. An editable PDF you can change, not a spreadsheet you have to maintain.

Send the order with one button

The merchant order comes off the same counts that built the job pack. Nothing retyped, nothing counted twice, nothing left off the docket.

They pick their own options

A live quote where the customer changes steel grade, gauge and guttering and watches the price move. No ringing you up to ask what it would cost.

On the roof

The measuring app goes up the ladder with you.

This is the part most quoting software doesn't have. Draw the roof on your phone or tablet while you're standing on it — and every ridge, hip, valley, barge and gutter is measured as you go, ready for the office before you're back in the ute.

  • Finger or pen, gloves on. Big targets, no fiddly menus, and it ignores your palm resting on the screen.
  • Lock the screen. One tap and the roof can't be redrawn by your leg while the phone's in your pocket.
  • Photos into the job. Shoot the whole roof in one go — every shot lands on the job, not in your camera roll.
  • No signal, no problem. It saves to the device and uploads itself the moment you have coverage.
  • The office picks it straight up. Site drafts arrive named and stamped with who measured it and on what.
Who built it

Written by roofers, on real jobs.

RoofMap came out of Flood Roofing, a working roofing company in Northland. It was built because quoting a re-roof took an afternoon of tape measures and spreadsheets, and because the material order was never quite right.

Every feature in it earned its place on a real job first. That's why the cut lists match how a roof is actually laid, and why the app works with one hand on a windy roof.

  • Priced and ordered on live New Zealand re-roofs, not demo data
  • Colorsteel®, Zincalume®, ColorCote® and Marley products already costed in
  • NZ pitches, NZ profiles, NZ suppliers, GST built in
