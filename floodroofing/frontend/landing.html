How it works

Four steps, one afternoon.

The same job that used to mean a ladder, a tape, a spreadsheet and three phone calls.

Draw the roof

Trace the outline over the aerial photo, set one known wall to scale, and every ridge, hip, valley, barge and gutter is measured for you.

Price it

Materials come off your own price book, labour off your own rates. Per roof, per building, with the margin in front of you as you work.

Send the quote

The customer gets a proper proposal on your own domain. They pick their steel, add a garage, and accept — you see it the moment they do.

Order and build

The material order emails to your supplier and the crew gets a job pack: sheet runs, flashing cut lists, back trays, fixings, all of it.

What it does

Everything between the site visit and the delivery.

Not a generic job-management app with roofing bolted on. Every part of it was built for one trade.

📐

Measurements you can order from

Gable, hip, Dutch gable, mono-pitch, stepped gutters, penetrations. Pitch-corrected, so what the plan says is what turns up on the truck.

📋

Cut lists that match the roof

Sheet runs laid out per plane, flashings itemised by type and length, back trays worked out from the penetrations you drew.

💲

Your prices, your margin

Your price book, your labour rates, your scaffold. The gross profit sits on screen while you price, not in a spreadsheet afterwards.

✍️

Quotes customers accept online

A real proposal with the roof plan on it. They choose their steel grade and guttering, watch the total move, and accept from their phone.

📶

Built for the roof, not the desk

Phone and tablet, pen or finger, gloves on. Out of range it saves to the device and uploads itself when the signal comes back.

👥

Your whole office, one account

Everyone shares the same jobs, price book and job numbers — and every job says who made it and who sent the order.

Who built it

Written by roofers, on real jobs.

RoofMap came out of Flood Roofing, a working roofing company in Northland. It was built because quoting a re-roof took an afternoon of tape measures and spreadsheets, and because the material order was never quite right.

Every feature in it earned its place on a real job first. That's why the cut lists match how a roof is actually laid, and why the app works with one hand on a windy roof.

  • Priced and ordered on live New Zealand re-roofs, not demo data
  • Colorsteel®, Zincalume®, ColorCote® and Marley products already costed in
  • NZ pitches, NZ profiles, NZ suppliers, GST built in
