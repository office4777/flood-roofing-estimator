Settings

      

      

      

      

      

      
Used to estimate still-to-buy materials for the cashflow + Expected A/P (same setting as Cash → deposit/materials %). Default 50%.

      
Materials are paid on the 20th of the month after they're ordered (supplier terms), and existing A/P lands on the next 20th.

      
      
On: estimates are grossed to GST-inclusive cash (to match Xero A/R + A/P) and a net GST payment to IRD is forecast — output GST on sales less the GST reclaimed on materials + supplier bills (COGS).

      
You file two-monthly (Apr–May paid ~28 Jun). Periods end May/Jul/Sep/Nov/Jan/Mar; each is paid the 28th of the following month.

      
Adds the GST you reclaim on GST-able overheads (power, fuel, tools…). Leave blank to reclaim GST on materials + A/P only (slightly overstates GST owed).

      
The current GST liability (for work already done) — settled on the next 28th. Blank = the GST account on the Xero balance sheet.

      
      
When on (and your opening balance is the live bank feed), invoices that already show up as bank deposits are treated as received — so a payment that's in the bank but not yet reconciled in Xero isn't counted twice.

⚙ Settings & sync

    

      
      
      
      
Refresh is the everyday one — recent jobs, current Xero P&L + cash, and dated labour for jobs that changed (seconds). Master refresh backfills the full history for every job + Xero to 2018 (minutes) — run at setup, then ~monthly. Both keep the screen awake and let you use the app while they run; Master saves as it goes, so if it's interrupted just tap it again to resume.

Export your active-jobs schedule as a CSV (from Excel: File → Save As → CSV) with a column for the scheduled finish date and, ideally, a job number or site/address to match on. The cashflow forecast then times each job's final invoice, materials and A/R off your real finish dates instead of the capacity estimate. Only dates within the next 3 months are used — anything further out is treated as tentative and falls back to the estimate. Manual payment-day picks still override.
